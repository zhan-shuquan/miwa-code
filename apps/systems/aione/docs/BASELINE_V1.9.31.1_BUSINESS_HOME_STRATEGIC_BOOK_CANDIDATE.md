# V1.9.31.1 Business Home Strategic Book Candidate

Status: candidate for production validation.

## Purpose

Separate content-reading pages from business-execution pages.

Business Home overview now uses a single strategic-publication visual language derived from the existing MIWA Group AI execution architecture deck:

- large cover page;
- A4-landscape-like screen canvas;
- one page, one primary idea;
- strong chapter hierarchy;
- three-to-five equal content columns when appropriate;
- generous whitespace;
- low interaction density;
- internal footer and page numbering;
- print/PDF layout aligned to A4 landscape.

## Boundary

This is a content-page rule, not a global business UI rule.

Operational pages such as orders, procurement, inventory, customer service, forms, tables and work execution continue to use the SaaS execution layout.

## Business Home overview pages

1. Cover
2. Why Business Home exists
3. Core and existing businesses
4. Incubating and planned businesses
5. Shared group capabilities

## Compatibility

All non-overview Business Home routes continue to use the V1.9.31 renderer. Sidebar, Aside, business entry routing, AI context and business content data are unchanged.

## Print

The overview uses A4 landscape page geometry with page breaks per publication page and exact print-color adjustment.
